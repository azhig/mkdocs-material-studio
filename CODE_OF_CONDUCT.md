# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Discuss code and ideas, not people.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. A wrong patch is usually a misunderstanding, not malice.
- Criticize the work, not its author, and say what exactly to change.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A project has a scope, and “not now” is
  a valid answer.
- Remember that English is a second language for many people here. Read past
  the wording to the meaning.

## What is not acceptable

Insults, harassment, discriminatory remarks, publishing someone's private
information, and persistently derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block accounts that break these rules. Serious or repeated cases lead to a
permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report privately through a
[security advisory](https://github.com/azhig/mkdocs-material-studio/security/advisories/new)
— it is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This document is a short adaptation of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
